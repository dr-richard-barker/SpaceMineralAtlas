Justification for each override:

  OSD-655 [calcium, potassium, magnesium, phosphorus, sulfur]
    stated: milligram_per_gram -> applied: microgram_per_gram
    Taking the stated mg/g at face value gives mean concentrations of 2943%
    (K), 805% (Mg), 651% (Ca), 551% (S) and 367% (P) of dry weight, which are
    physically impossible. Interpreted as ug/g the same values agree with
    OSD-745, whose lettuce elemental data are independently expressed in
    mg/kg: Ca 5.83 vs 4.50 mg/g, K 29.4 vs 47.9 mg/g, Mg 8.05 vs 2.60 mg/g.
    The three elements OSD-655 labels in ug/g (Al, B, Cu, Fe, Mn, Zn) require
    no correction and already agree across studies, which localises the defect
    to the five mg/g labels. OSD-655_dictionary_DataDictionary_OSD-655.csv
    repeats the incorrect unit, so the defect is in the curation metadata, not
    only the header.
